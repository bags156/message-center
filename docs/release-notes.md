Message Center 1.5.32.

- Swiping left to see when messages were sent no longer flashes. The whole conversation slides as one, so a new message or read receipt arriving mid-swipe can't snap it back, and your friends' bubbles slide off the side instead of being cut off at an invisible line.
- Cropping a photo before you send it works now.
  - The photo has room around it, so the corners are no longer at the edge of the screen where a drag counts as Back. The corners are also protected from the Back swipe.
  - The corners and sides have bigger handles, you can drag a side on its own, and thirds lines show while you drag.
  - Cancel, Undo and Done sit below the status bar and camera, so tapping them works. Cancel still asks before discarding your edits.
- In the full-screen photo viewer, Close, Save and Share also sit below the status bar.
- The ✕ for removing a photo you've picked is easier to hit.

Install over the existing app through Obtainium. Your pairing, conversations and settings are retained.
