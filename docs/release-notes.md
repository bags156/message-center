More features, fewer surprises.

- **Shared locations** now show as a card with the place and a map; tap to open it in Maps. (Updated the Google Messages library, which also brings its connection fixes.)
- **Group events** appear in chats: who joined or left, group renames, and when a chat becomes end-to-end encrypted.
- **Mention alerts.** Add your name in Settings → Mention alerts. When someone writes @YourName in a group, you're notified even if that chat is muted or snoozed.
- **Snooze a chat** for 1 hour, 8 hours, or until tomorrow morning (long-press it in the list, or in its menu or details). Snoozed chats show 💤.
- **Undo send.** Text messages wait 5 seconds before sending; tap "Sending… • Tap to undo" to take one back. Turn it off in Settings.
- **Undo delete.** Deleting a conversation hides it with an Undo button for a few seconds before it's removed from your phone.
- **Voice message speed:** tap 1× on a voice message for 1.5× or 2×.
- **Unknown senders:** ⋮ → Unknown senders lists chats with numbers that aren't in your contacts.
- **Remind me:** long-press a message → Remind me… (in an hour, tonight, tomorrow, or a time you pick). The reminder opens the chat at that message.
- **Export a chat:** chat details → Export chat to a file (saved in Downloads/Message Center, with a Share option).
- **Nicknames:** chat details → Set a nickname (or tap a group member). Shown in the app, notifications, and the widget, on this phone only.
- **Safer link previews:** previews are only fetched for contacts and chats you've written in, so strangers can't tell when you've seen their link.
- **Safer sharing:** files shared into the app must come from another app; it no longer accepts raw file paths or its own private files.

Install over 0.7.2; the package and signing key are unchanged.
