Message Center 1.5.31.

- Copying a link from a message that has other words in it now works.
  - Press and hold the link itself to Open, Copy or Share just that link. For a phone number you get Call, Text or Copy, and for an email address you get Email or Copy.
  - Holding the link no longer opens it when you lift your finger.
  - Holding anywhere else in the message gives the usual message options, which now include Copy link (one for each of the first three links).

Install over the existing app through Obtainium. Your pairing, conversations and settings are retained.
