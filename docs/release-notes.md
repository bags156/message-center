Message Center 1.5.75.

- See what you wrote. A message translated before it went now says "Sent in Spanish · Show what you wrote" under it. Tap that to see your original, and tap again to see what was sent. What you wrote stays on this phone only.
- Check before sending (optional). Turn it on in a chat's Details → Sending language. Tapping Send then shows the translation, with what you wrote under it. From there, Send sends it, Edit keeps your draft, and Send as written sends what you typed without translating it.
- Send goes first. A message you're sending, or one you asked to translate, no longer waits behind automatic translation of messages you've received. Those step aside and finish afterwards.
- Faster compact translation. The compact translator keeps its language models loaded between messages, instead of loading them again for every message (twice for languages that go through English). They're released after 30 seconds idle, or when you leave the app.
- Language packs ask before using mobile data. On mobile data, a pack that isn't on the phone yet no longer downloads by itself. When you're sending or translating a message, you're asked: Download now, Always, or Wait for Wi-Fi. Automatic translation of received messages waits for Wi-Fi, and a tap on its status line downloads the pack now. To always allow mobile data, go to Settings → Translation → Language packs on mobile data.
- Pictures download as they arrive. A picture that fails to download, or that your phone hasn't handed over yet, is tried again after 30 seconds, 2 minutes and 10 minutes. Pictures from the last few days that never downloaded are fetched when the connection or network comes back, so they're ready when you open the chat instead of waiting for a tap. Full-quality photos and Android's Data Saver still control this.

Update through Obtainium over the existing app. Your pairing, messages, chat language choices and downloaded language packs are retained.
