Message Center 1.5.22.

- Notes: a private notepad for each person and group, for gift ideas, birthdays, and things to follow up on. Open it from a chat's ⋮ menu (Notes) or from Details. Notes save as you type, stay on this phone, and are never sent.
- Once you've written notes about someone, their chat shows a notes button at the top.
- Long-press a message and tap Add to notes to save it as a dated line. In a group, it goes to the notes about whoever sent it.
- In a group's details, tap a member, then Notes about them. A person's notes are the same everywhere you see them.
- Inbox search also looks through your notes.

Install over the existing app through Obtainium. Your pairing, conversations and settings are retained.
