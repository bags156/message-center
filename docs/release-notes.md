Message Center 1.5.63.

- Making a group, redone:
  - Picking people: the people you pick show as chips under the search box. Tap a chip to take them back out. Picking someone no longer jumps the list back to the top. Picking from a search empties it, ready for the next person. The button counts down: "Pick 1 more person", then "Next · 3 people".
  - Naming it: Next opens a screen of its own instead of a pop-up. It shows the group's picture, a name box, and who's in it. You can take someone out or go back to add more, and the name you typed is kept. While the group is being created, the button says "Creating…". If something goes wrong, the reason stays on screen so you can try again.
- Someone known only by their number now shows a person outline instead of the number's first digit, in lists and in group pictures.
- Renaming a group: tap the group's name at the top of its details (it has a pencil now). The new sheet matches the rest of the app. Save shows it's working, and any problem stays in view.

Install over the existing app through Obtainium. Your pairing, conversations and settings are retained.
