Message Center 1.5.51.

- Android Auto: Message Center's chat notifications now show in the car. Android Auto reads them aloud and sends the reply you speak, and the car's assistant can read them and reply too. If Google Messages also notifies you in the car, turn its notifications off in Android Auto's settings so you don't hear each message twice.
- Birthdays: from your contacts, a note at the top of someone's chat the week before their birthday and on the day ("Hannah turns 30 today"), with "Say happy birthday" (the balloons come too), and a 🎂 by their name in the list on the day. This needs the contacts permission, the same one names and photos use. ✕ hides the note until next year.
- Package tracking: a message with a UPS, USPS, FedEx or Amazon tracking number gets a "Track" card that opens the carrier's tracking page. Hold it to copy the number.
- Edited messages show "Edited", like in Google Messages.
- Editing: Google only lets messages be edited in its own Messages app, not on linked devices like Message Center. So holding one of your RCS messages from the last 15 minutes offers "Edit in Google Messages": it copies the words and opens that chat there, where you hold the message and tap the pencil. The change shows here with "Edited".

Install over the existing app through Obtainium. Your pairing, conversations and settings are retained.
