Message Center 1.0.9.

**GIFs and stickers move**
- GIFs and animated stickers now play right in the chat and in the full-screen viewer, instead of showing only their first frame.

**Start at what you haven't read**
- When a chat has more unread messages than fit on the screen, it opens at the first one, under a "New messages" line, with ↓ to jump to the newest. The line goes away once you reply.

**Contact cards**
- A shared contact shows as a card with the name and number, plus **Save contact**. Tap it to call, email, or open it in another app.

**Verification codes clean themselves up**
- Verification-code texts more than a day old are deleted automatically, in Google Messages too. Only codes from businesses (senders not in your contacts), never group chats. Turn it off in Settings → Notifications → Delete codes after a day.

**Report a problem**
- Settings → About → Report a problem makes one file with what went wrong (connection history, the app's error log, your phone model, no message text), plus the picture or video that wouldn't open if you like. Send it however you want. "Can't show this photo" also has a Report button.

Install over 1.0.8; the package and signing key are unchanged.
