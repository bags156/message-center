Message Center 1.5.36.

Messages that don't go through now say why, and send themselves again when it's safe to.

- **Waiting for signal • sends automatically.** It couldn't reach Google at all (weak or no connection), so it definitely hasn't gone anywhere. It tries again by itself, more often at first and then less often, and right away when your signal improves. This works for photos too, and for replies from notifications.
- **Sent to Google • waiting to confirm delivery.** Google has it, but delivery hasn't been confirmed yet. It's not resent automatically, because it could still go out and they'd get it twice. It clears by itself once delivery is confirmed. If it's still unconfirmed 10 minutes after the connection's back, it changes to "Not confirmed • Tap to send again".
- **Couldn't send • will retry when you have signal.** Your phone tried but the text didn't go (usually no cell signal). Texts sent from Message Center in the last hour go again once by themselves: when mobile data starts working again, or after 10 minutes. It's only ever once, and only for the general "couldn't send" failure, not for "too large" or "they can't get RCS".
- Messages waiting on the connection say what they're waiting for: "Waiting for signal", "Waiting for Google Messages" or "Waiting to reconnect".
