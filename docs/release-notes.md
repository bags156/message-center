Message Center 1.5.30.

- New: Settings → Notifications → On the lock screen. Choose what notifications show while your phone is locked. Same as when unlocked, Name only (who it's from, not what they said), or Nothing (just "New message"). Unlock and the full message shows.
- It works however your phone's own lock screen setting is set. Notifications are redrawn as the screen turns off and again when you unlock. Unread reminders and message reminders follow it too.
