Message Center 1.5.34.

- Chat themes have a new Custom… option (⋮ → Theme in any chat).
  - Pick any color with the Color, Intensity and Brightness sliders, or type a color code like #E91E63. A sample chat shows your bubble and send button as you drag.
  - Your bubbles are exactly the color you pick. In the other look (light vs. dark) they get a matching shade, the words on them are light on bright colors and dark on pale ones, so they always read, and the send button is adjusted just enough to stand out.
  - Colors you've used are kept under Recent, so you can give other chats the same one.

Install over the existing app through Obtainium. Your pairing, conversations and settings are retained.
