Message Center 1.5.86.

- Turn a message into a task. Long-press a message and tap Create task. The first time, pick your task app: the task apps on your phone are listed (Google Tasks, Todoist, TickTick, Microsoft To Do, Keep and others), or choose Another app… for any app that takes text. The task's title is the message's first line, and its notes have the whole message plus who sent it and when. A photo or video on its own becomes "Photo from …".
- Your pick is remembered, so after that the menu says "Create task in" your app. Change it any time in Settings › Messages › Tasks.

Update through Obtainium over the existing app. Your pairing, messages and settings are retained.
