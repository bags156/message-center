Message Center 1.5.77.

- Messages you type in Spanish go as written. In a chat that translates what you send into Spanish, a message you've already written in Spanish is sent as you typed it, without translating or a check. The same goes for any language a chat sends in. Short messages need the phone to be very sure. A word or two, such as "ok" or "sí", is translated as before.
- What you send through the stronger translator sounds like a text from a person. It uses tú instead of usted, Latin American Spanish and everyday wording (“¿Puedes…?”, not “¿Podrías…?”), and keeps your emoji. It writes one gender form instead of both (agotado, not agotado/a), and an opening ¡ or ¿ always gets its ! or ?. If the casual wording would change a link, code, time or emoji, it tries once more with the plain wording. Messages you read keep the more literal translation, which was more accurate on slang.
- It's quicker. The stronger translator remembers its setup between messages, for both directions at once (reading their Spanish, sending your English), so after the first message each one is quicker. It stays ready for 3 minutes after you translate, or 1 minute after you leave the app.

Update through Obtainium over the existing app. Your pairing, messages and settings are retained.
