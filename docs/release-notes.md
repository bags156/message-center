Message Center 1.5.60.

- Chat heads stack: several floating chats now sit in one stack at the side of the screen, with the newest on top, two more peeking out below, and one red count for all their new messages. Tap the stack to spread them out, then tap a head to open its chat. Tap anywhere else, or leave them for a few seconds, and they stack again. A single head opens straight away, as before.
- Closing chat heads works: dragging onto the ✕ now goes by where the ✕ really is on your screen. Before, it could miss even when you dropped right on it. The ✕ turns red, with a buzz, when letting go will close. Drag the stack there to close them all. When they're spread out, drag one head there to close just that chat. Android's edge swipe for Back also no longer takes a drag that starts on a head at the side.

Install over the existing app through Obtainium. Your pairing, conversations and settings are retained.
