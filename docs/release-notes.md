Message Center 1.5.87.

- Keep chats off Android Auto. Long-press a chat in your inbox and turn off Show in Android Auto (it's also under Notifications in the chat's own menu). That chat's messages won't show or be read out in the car. They still notify on your phone as usual, as a plain notification while you're connected to the car.
- If Google Messages' own notifications are turned on, Android Auto can still show that app's copy of the message. Turn those off in Google Messages to keep a chat fully off the car.

Update through Obtainium over the existing app. Your pairing, messages and settings are retained.
