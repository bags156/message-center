Message Center 1.5.5.

- Rename a group: open an RCS group's details and tap Rename group…. Everyone in the group sees the new name.
- Group photo on this phone: pick a picture for a group from its details. It only shows in this app, not for anyone else.
- Text when I leave: arrival texts can now go when you leave a place instead of when you get there.
- Check-ins: for a one-time arrival text, turn on "If I'm not there by…" and pick a time. If you haven't arrived by then, a second text goes ("Not home yet, running late." by default). Getting there cancels it.
- Video quality: Fast (up to 720p), Standard (up to 1080p) or Full (the original when it's under 100 MB). Change it next to the attachment strip when a video is attached, or in Settings → Video quality. Picture messages still squeeze videos under 1 MB.
- Not possible yet: adding or removing people, leaving a group, or changing a group's photo for everyone. Google's linked-device connection doesn't offer those.

Install over the existing app through Obtainium. Your pairing, conversations and settings are retained.
