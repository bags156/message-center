Message Center 1.5.59.

- "Sent to Google • waiting to confirm delivery" no longer sticks around for a text that went. That label means Google didn't answer when the app sent it, so the app can't tell whether it went. Now the app checks the chat with Google 10 seconds and a minute later, and the label goes as soon as the text's own copy turns up. If it never does, it still says "Not confirmed • Tap to send again" after 10 minutes, so nothing is sent twice without you choosing to.

Install over the existing app through Obtainium. Your pairing, conversations and settings are retained.
