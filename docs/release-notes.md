Message Center 1.5.68.

- Transcripts fold. Tap Hide under a voice message's transcript, or tap the transcript itself, to fold it to one line: "Transcript: the first words… · Show". Tap Show to open it again. Each transcript stays the way you left it, and a transcript that stopped partway folds the same way.
- Transcripts are searchable. A word someone said in a voice message now finds it, both in the inbox's search and in a conversation's search. It shows as "Voice message: …the words around it…", and tapping it goes to that voice message. Only voice messages that have been transcribed can be found this way.

Install over the existing app through Obtainium. Your pairing, conversations and settings are retained.
