Message Center 1.5.52.

- Suggested replies: when someone texts you, a few replies to tap ("Sounds good!", "On my way") show above the message box, in your chat's color, and on the notification's Reply (so in Android Auto too). Tapping one in the app puts it in the box to send or change. They come from Android itself, on your phone: nothing is sent anywhere. They're English-first, and they don't show when a chat's notifications hide what was said. Turn them off in Settings → Messages → Suggested replies.
- Translate: hold a message and tap Translate. Where your phone's Android can translate on the phone, the translation shows under the message ("Translated from Spanish · Show original"); otherwise it opens in Google Translate's pop-up over the chat. Messages already in your language just say so.
