Message Center 1.5.40.

- Songs open in your music app. When someone sends a song, album or artist from Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, Tidal, Deezer, Amazon Music or SoundCloud, the preview gets "Open in…" with your app's name. It opens the same song in your app, even when the link is from a different service. The first tap asks which app you use; change it in Settings → Appearance → Music app, or by holding the preview.
- It says how close the match is: "Same song" when it found the exact song, or "Search" when it opens your app's search for it instead. A live version or a remix is never taken for the original.
- Podcasts work the same way, with their own app (Settings → Podcast app): Spotify, YouTube Music, Pocket Casts, Podcast Addict, AntennaPod, Amazon Music, or Apple Podcasts on the web.
- Each song is looked up once, in the background, when it arrives from someone you know, so it's ready when you open the chat. The lookup uses free public music catalogs (Apple's, Deezer's, MusicBrainz and YouTube Music's search). Only the song's name and codes are sent, never your messages.

Install over the existing app through Obtainium. Your pairing, conversations and settings are retained.
