Message Center 1.5.57.

- See-through chat heads: Settings → Notifications → Floating chats → Message Center's chat heads. Chats float as round heads at the side of the screen, as see-through as you like while they're left alone (the "Strength when left alone" slider), and come up to full strength when you touch one or a new message arrives. Android draws its own bubbles at full strength and doesn't let apps change that, so these are Message Center's own.
- Tap a head to open the chat in a floating window over whatever you're doing (tap outside it, or Back, to close). Drag the heads to move them (they settle at the nearer side), or onto the ✕ at the bottom to close them. A red count shows new messages.
- The same chats float as before (Android's bubble settings still choose which), and "Open as bubble" opens a head. Chat heads need "Display over other apps"; the app asks for it when you choose them.

Install over the existing app through Obtainium. Your pairing, conversations and settings are retained.
