Message Center 1.5.77.

- Messages you type in Spanish go as written. In a chat that translates what you send into Spanish, a message you've already written in Spanish is sent as you typed it, without translating or a check. The same goes for any language a chat sends in. Short messages need the phone to be very sure. A word or two, such as "ok" or "sí", is translated as before.
- The stronger translator writes the way people text. It uses tú instead of usted, Latin American Spanish, everyday English ("Who's picking up the kids?", "I'm on my way"), and keeps your emoji. In Spanish, it writes one gender form instead of both (agotado, not agotado/a), and an opening ¡ or ¿ always gets its ! or ?. If the casual wording would change a link, code, time or emoji, it tries once more with the plain wording.
- It's quicker. The stronger translator remembers its setup between messages, so the next message in the same direction is about twice as fast. It stays ready for 3 minutes after you translate, or 1 minute after you leave the app.

Update through Obtainium over the existing app. Your pairing, messages and settings are retained.
